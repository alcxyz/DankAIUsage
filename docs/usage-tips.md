# Usage tips

## Timing a Codex subscription upgrade

One observed account upgrade on 2026-09-08 moved from a 5x subscription
($100) to a 20x subscription ($200) while 18% of the weekly allowance
remained. The upgrade unexpectedly reset the weekly allowance, while the
account's available banked resets remained available.

This is a single user-reported experience, not a guarantee of current pricing,
billing behavior, or OpenAI policy. If you already plan to upgrade, you may
want to consider your remaining allowance and upgrade timing. There is no
evidence that waiting for exactly 1% remaining is optimal: task timing and
billing considerations may matter more than preserving a small remainder. Do
not upgrade solely in expectation of an allowance reset, and do not depend on
banked resets carrying over.
