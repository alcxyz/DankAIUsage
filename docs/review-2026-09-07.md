# UI and listing review — 2026-09-07

Scope: review the existing v0.3.0 interface before another release or registry
submission, using DankCalendar and DankDiskUsage as presentation references.

## What works

- Provider logos follow the DMS accent in the supplied dropdown and bar images.
- The bar shows the selected Claude quotas alongside Codex without losing a
  provider. Icon choices and quota choices have their own settings sections.
- The dropdown separates providers, lists scoped limits, shows monetary usage,
  and directs users to the provider to consume available resets.
- The helper and widget retain provider-defined quota buckets rather than
  inventing a fixed pair of windows (ADR-0009).

## Findings to resolve before final screenshots

1. **Resolved locally: progress and label direction differed.** `quotaProgress()` filled the bar
   with `percentUsed`, while `quotaValue()` labels remaining percentage. In the
   supplied screenshot, 26% left appears next to a bar roughly three-quarters
   full. Both numbers are correct, but the relationship is easy to misread.
   ADR-0002 makes remaining allowance the primary metric. Prefer remaining
   fill for subscription windows, or explicitly label used/left; keep monetary
   spend semantics explicit. The implemented Left / Used toggle now aligns
   every quota's percentage and fill, including credits, and persists the
   selection. Expandable bar controls expose compact/icon/Claude quota settings.
2. **Ordinary information looks like an error.** `providerNote()` combines the
   normal local-history caveat, prime status, and authentication failures in
   one amber text area. Give routine history information neutral styling and
   reserve warning styling for actionable failures. This would also make a
   signed-out account more noticeable.
3. **Account recovery is indirect.** A present CLI sets `available=true` even
   when its account is signed out. The empty quota section then relies on a
   technical note to explain why. A clear sign-in instruction and retry state
   would improve the empty state. Documented the existing recovery procedure;
   no credential handling was changed in this review.
4. **Overview wording is technical.** “6 quota buckets” describes the data
   structure. “6 limits” would be clearer to users. The provider header and
   top bar could also make “left” explicit, especially for credit percentages.
5. **Claude summary has a fixed green color.** Logos respect the theme, but
   `providerColor()` hardcodes green for Claude and red for failures. Prefer
   semantic theme colors in a future UI pass, with actual quota severity
   reflected consistently.
